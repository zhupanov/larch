## Independently mergeable constraint (mandatory)

Each proposed piece must be a **self-contained PR** that can land on `main` **before** its dependents are designed or implemented. If piece A must merge before piece B, express that as **A blocks B** (B is blocked-by A). **Circular** blocker relationships are forbidden — verify the directed dependency graph is acyclic.

## Piece count guidance

Recommend **2–5** independently mergeable pieces (soft guidance). If you believe **more than five** pieces are warranted, justify explicitly in your response.
